### What is Linux?

Linux is a branch of free and open source operating systems, engineered by people - for the people. You as an individual (not as a company or body of authority) can look at the source code for the linux kernel and personally verify its structure, and even modify it to your hearts content.

### Why don't more people use it?

Linux for many years - due to the technical nature of the people who develop it, has been considered unfriendly to users - obtuse some might say. I think this is a genuine tragedy, since when the operating system is configured correctly, the sky is genuinely the limit. I've personally boiled down the reasons people don't switch to the following:

- Hesitance to install a new OS on their SSD's
- Scared of the terminal bricking their system
- App support
- Hardware support

**thankfully** in the year 2026 - the vast majority of these issues have become much easier to approach. The installers for linux are alot more approachable and user friendly, and beyond that - developers have come a massive way from the original premise of developing on linux through the terminal and the vast majority of projects are now easily configured through user friendly UI's.

#### Ensuring your system is healthy

As an active developer and linux user, its always important to understand exactly what your system is running, so you can maximize battery life and performance.

Using Btop, can allow you to see all of the following metrics:
- Active CPU usage
- Wattage 
- Memory usage

I highly recommend taking a look at least once per session at this, just to make sure everything is fine!
